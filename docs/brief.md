# Topic brief (verbatim from handout, photographed 2026-10-14)

## Medical Clinic Registration

**Registering for a clinic with the right information**

**Main user:** Patient, such as a pensioner attending a special clinic

**Problem**

Patients may need to register at a hospital, provide personal details again, bring previous medical records and find out whether the required specialist is available. This can create delays and make it harder to arrange the right appointment.

(Handout photo: a hospital registration counter, nurse at a laptop and printer, patients queuing behind glass.)

## Our reading (not part of the handout)

Three pains: re-entering identity details, carrying old records, not knowing specialist availability. Each maps to a DPI: SLUDI (identity), NDX (consented records/civil data), plus appointment/availability, payment and a verifiable credential (clinic registration card) for the wallet.
